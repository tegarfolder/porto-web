Approach

Good motion starts before animation.

I believe the animation should be clear before it is beautiful.

  1. 01

    Clarify the message

    What this is actually trying to say, before anything is designed to look good.

  2. 02

    Structure the story

    The order matters as much as the words: script and story beats come first.

  3. 03

    Visualize the idea

    Style frames and a visual system, so the idea has a defined look before it moves.

  4. 04

    Animate what matters

    Motion goes where it earns attention, not everywhere it's technically possible.

How it runs

What happens after you say yes? The same four steps, every time, with no surprises in between.

01

Script

What are we actually trying to say?

I write it, you approve it. Nothing gets animated against a sentence nobody believes.

02

Storyboard

What should the audience see, and in what order?

Key frames and a rough timing pass, so you can see the shape of the film before it costs anything to change.

03

Animation

How should the idea move?

First cut, one round of notes, final. Extra rounds are fine (they're quoted, not absorbed silently).

04

Delivery

How does the final film need to work in the real world?

Master file, the cuts you need, captions, and the project archive.

Good to know Most projects run three to six weeks from approved script. Rush is possible and priced as rush. I'll tell you when a shorter film would do the job better than the one you asked for.

Services

Four modes. Each one starts with a script and a storyboard, because that is where a film is actually won or lost.

Typically 60–120 seconds

Explainer

For products whose value is invisible until someone shows it. I write the script and get it agreed before a frame is drawn, then animate. You get the film, captions, and a square and vertical cut.

Typically 30–60 seconds

Product

Feature launches and interface films. I work from your real screens and retime them so each state holds long enough to read. Delivered in the aspect ratios your launch actually needs.

Typically 15–30 seconds

Event

Opens, stings, and stage loops. Built to the venue's resolution and running order, seamless on loop, and legible from the back of the room.

Project-based

Brand

Motion systems rather than single films: how the mark enters and exits, which transitions are allowed, what the timing curve is. Delivered as guidelines plus source files your team can keep using.

Quote Pricing depends on length, complexity, and how much of the script already exists. Send what you have and you'll get a range back, not a form.
