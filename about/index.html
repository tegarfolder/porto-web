About

A motion designer who would rather cut thirty seconds than add thirty more.

I'm Putra Tegar, a motion designer working across five modes: explainer films, product films, event opens, brand motion systems, and self-initiated exploration. You work with me directly — the person who writes the script is the person who animates it.

Most of the work arrives the same way. Someone has a product that makes sense once you've used it and makes no sense at all in a sentence. Or a launch date and screens that don't yet move. Or a logo that needs to behave consistently across forty different touchpoints. The animation is the last part of that job, not the first — so every project runs write it, board it, then animate it.

I say no to briefs I'd do badly. If a film isn't the right answer, or the budget won't reach the idea, I'll say so before you've spent anything.

The exploration work is how the client work stays current — technique tests and style frames made with no brief attached. It's usually where a new approach shows up first.
