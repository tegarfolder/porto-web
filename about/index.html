About

A motion graphics studio that would rather cut thirty seconds than add thirty more.

Putra Tegar is a motion graphics studio working out of Jakarta, remote by default. We make four kinds of film — explainer, product, event, and brand — and we make them the same way each time: write it, board it, then animate it.

Most of the work arrives the same way. Someone has a product that makes sense once you've used it and makes no sense at all in a sentence. Or a launch date and screens that don't yet move. Or a logo that needs to behave consistently across forty different touchpoints. The animation is the last part of that job, not the first.

We say no to briefs we'd do badly. If a film isn't the right answer, or the budget won't reach the idea, we'll say so before you've spent anything.
